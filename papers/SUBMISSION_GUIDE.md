# ICEIR 2026 — Submission Guide

The 3rd International Conference on Intelligent Computing and Efficient Information Retrieval (ICEIR 2026)
- Host: Nam Can Tho University, Can Tho City, Vietnam
- Conference date: Dec 12th 2026
- Website: https://iceir.nctu.edu.vn/

## SUBMISSION SYSTEM

Paper's submission system used at ICEIR 2026 abides by Microsoft CMT. All submission processes will be
conducted using the Microsoft CMT system.

- Submission portal: https://cmt3.research.microsoft.com/ICEIR2026
- Microsoft CMT Guide [in English]:
  https://iceir.nctu.edu.vn/assets/file/[EN] GUIDE TO SUBMITTING PAPERS ON THE MICROSOFT CMT3 SYSTEM.pdf
- Microsoft CMT Guide [in Vietnamese]:
  https://iceir.nctu.edu.vn/assets/file/[VN] HƯỚNG DẪN NỘP BÀI TRÊN HỆ THỐNG MICROSOFT CMT3.pdf

## TEMPLATE

The paper template for ICEIR 2026 adheres to Springer's standard format.

- Springer LNNS format (Information for authors and editors):
  https://link.springer.com/series/558/information-for-authors-and-editors
- Use the LaTeX template rather than the Word template. If two papers are of similar quality,
  preference will be given to the paper prepared in LaTeX.
- If selected for JSDE or NIRJ, authors will need to resubmit using the respective
  JSDE template or NIRJ template.

## SUBMISSION GUIDELINES

- Manuscripts must be written in English and be 12–15 pages long.
- Papers must be original, not previously published, and not currently under consideration elsewhere.
- Submit online in PDF format via Microsoft CMT.

## PUBLICATION

- Top 39% (based on review results): Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems (LNNS), Springer,
  indexed by Scopus (Q3).
- Next 20%: proceedings with an ISBN.
- Selected papers may be considered for special issues of the Journal of Science and Economic
  Development (JSED), ISSN: 2588-1272, after at least 40% revision and extension.

## REVIEWING

- Peer-reviewed; each paper reviewed by at least three PC members.
- Acceptance rate: ~30% for research papers published in the proceedings.

## REGISTRATION FEE

- Paper published in Springer LNNS: USD 250 per paper (at least one author must register).
- Paper published in an ISBN Book: free of charge.
